## Defensive contributions (unchanged from 2025/26)

- **DEF:** 10+ CBIT (clearances, blocks, interceptions, tackles) → +2
- **MID and FWD:** 12+ CBIRT (adds ball recoveries) → +2
- Capped at +2 per player per match. Cannot be earned twice regardless of volume.
- Modelling note: threshold-crossing is binary, so per-match distribution matters far more than the season mean. A player averaging 9.5 CBIT is worth much less than one averaging 11, and two players on the same average can differ by several points a season on variance alone.

## What changed for 2026/27

1. **Bonus points system rebalanced** — the material change. Being tackled no longer costs BPS, which lifts dribblers. Clearances, blocks and interceptions cut from 1 BPS per 2 actions to 1 per 3, reducing overlap with DefCon. Goalkeeper saves restructured: 2 BPS for any save, +1 more for a save inside the box (3 total), +1 for saving a big chance, penalty saves 8. Net effect: better bonus prospects for keepers, attacking full-backs and attackers; worse for stationary centre-backs who were double-dipping BPS and DefCon.

   **Quantified, added 19 Aug 2026.** Fantasy Football Scout re-ran 2025/26 under the new weights. Gabriel is the single biggest loser, dropping from 30 bonus points to 20, and **no centre-back gained more than one extra bonus**. Tarkowski, Bijol and Thiaw also lose. The 24 players who would have gained three or more were "almost exclusively full-backs, goalkeepers and attacking midfielders", with Lewis-Potter and Kudus named. Most-tackled players from 2025/26 (Rogers 156, Bowen 143, Semenyo 137, Aaronson 127, Ndiaye 126, Doku 113) stop paying the tackle tax, though that only converts to actual bonus for players who already finish near the top three.

   Strategic read: prefer full-backs and ball-playing defenders over stationary centre-halves at the same price, and prefer save-volume keepers on mid-table sides over set-and-forget cheap keepers behind strong defences. Treat the market as only **partly** repriced, since 2026/27 prices were set off 2025/26 points.
2. **Live bonus and live ranks** — projected bonus applied from the 20th minute of each match; mini-league tables and overall rank update in-play.
3. **Later score lockdown** — gameweek scores finalise at 09:00 UK time the day after the final match, not one hour after the whistle. More Opta review time, so provisional DefCon and bonus can still move overnight.
4. **No AFCON free transfers** — AFCON shifts to June/July 2027, so the December bonus transfers of previous seasons do not apply.
5. **Percentage rank tracking, price change predictor, rookie league, squad selection assistant** — UI additions, no scoring impact. The price predictor is worth cross-checking our own snapshot-derived model against.

## Unchanged

- £100.0m budget, 15 players (2 GK / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 3 FWD), max 3 per club.
- 1 free transfer per GW, rolling to a maximum of 5. −4 per extra transfer. Max 20 transfers in a single GW.
- 50% sell-on fee on profit.
- Two full chip sets: Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, Bench Boost. First set expires **13:30 GMT Sat 2 Jan 2027**. Second set available from GW20.
- Deadline is 90 minutes before the first kickoff of the gameweek.

## Position reclassifications (11 total)

Notable: Kroupi FWD→MID (gains, playing as a 10). Marmoush MID→FWD (loses midfield scoring and clean sheets). Dorgu DEF→MID (loses clean sheets and DefCon-at-10). Wieffer, Sessegnon and Lewis-Potter all moved into defence (now earn clean sheets and the friendlier 10-CBIT threshold).

## Key dates and calendar shape

- **GW1 deadline: Fri 21 Aug 2026 17:30 UTC = Sat 22 Aug 05:30 NZST**
- First chip set expires: Sat 2 Jan 2027, 13:30 GMT (the GW19 deadline)
- Second chip set opens: GW20
- Season ends 30 May 2027

**2026/27 calendar structure (added 19 Aug 2026).** The season starts a week later than usual because of the expanded 48-team World Cup. It runs as **33 weekends plus five midweek rounds**. The September international break is an **extended three-week window** and the October window has been **removed**, so GW6 is the first gameweek after the break. Over Christmas and New Year no two rounds fall within 60 hours, and Boxing Day is a Saturday with a fuller slate. **Nine clubs are in Europe, five in the Champions League**, which raises rotation risk in midweek-adjacent gameweeks.

**Blank and double gameweeks for the first half are not yet known.** They depend on FA Cup and EFL Cup draws. Any chip plan tied to a specific double before those draws is speculative, so a first-half Bench Boost must be justified as a "field a full 15" play rather than a double-gameweek play.
